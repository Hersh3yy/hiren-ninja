The midnight maths

Which day is a 1 a.m. party?

A party that starts Friday at 1 a.m. does not feel like Friday. It feels like Thursday night that went late. A calendar disagrees, and that is the puzzle.

The calendar says

Friday, 01:00

→

The planner shows

Thursday, at "25:00"

The rule: any party starting before 6 a.m. is moved to the night before. So your timetable just keeps counting past 24:00 instead of chopping one party across two days.

Nights belong to the evening they start in. Ask Nami: if a party starts at 3 a.m. Saturday, which night is it really part of, and what time would the planner show? (Friday night, 27:00.)
