Inside the radar

Your playlist's four stops

STOP 1

You paste a link

A playlist from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal or Deezer. Or just type DJ names.

STOP 2

Reader

Opens the playlist and writes down every artist in it.

STOP 3

Matcher

Checks your artists against the whole ADE line-up. Forgives typos: "did you mean?"

STOP 4

Your parties

Shows the days, clubs, tickets and prices for every match.

The live planner uses no AI, just careful matching. The one AI touch happened earlier and once: a helper AI read every daytime talk and sorted it into 16 topics, which the planner then just reads off a list.

Four stops: link in, artists out, matched to the line-up, parties shown. Point out the one place AI was used, and that it was done once, not every visit. Ask Nami: at which stop could a typo in a DJ's name cause trouble?
