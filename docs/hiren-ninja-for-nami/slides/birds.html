The playful bit

The birds that follow your mouse

Live, not a video

On the home page a flock of birds drifts across the screen, and they swirl when you move your mouse. The computer draws every bird fresh, frame by frame, with a 3D drawing kit. It is never the same twice.

Programmers' kit: Three.js, with a helper called Vanta.

One toy we took away

There used to be a glowing light-sculpture toy you could play with. The longer you left it open, the more it slowly clogged up your computer. So Hiren removed it. Better to lose a toy than slow your machine.

The birds are drawn live, so they react to you. Ask Nami: would you rather a page be calm and still, or always moving? When does moving get annoying?
