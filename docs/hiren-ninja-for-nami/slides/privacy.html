Being fair to you

It counts, but it never knows you

What it keeps

Plain tallies: how many times an artist was found, how many searches happened. Numbers, not names.

What it never keeps

No account, no login, no name, no "who you are." It cannot tell one visitor from another.

Your saved plan lives in your own browser, on your own device, not on Hiren's computer. Clear your browser and it is gone. He never had it.

The app learns that people like something, never who those people are, like a clicker counter at a door that counts heads but takes no photos. Ask Nami: what is a fair thing a website should be allowed to count, and what should it never store?
