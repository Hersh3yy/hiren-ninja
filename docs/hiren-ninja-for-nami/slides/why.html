Why bother

Why make your own website?

The old way

You only live inside other people's apps, like Instagram or LinkedIn. They pick the rules, the look, and what shows up. You are a guest in someone else's house.

The new way

Your own address: hiren.ninja. You own it. It looks exactly how you want, and it is really you, not an app pretending to be you.

A personal website is a place online that is yours, not rented from a big app. Ask Nami: name one thing an app could change tomorrow that you would not be able to stop.
