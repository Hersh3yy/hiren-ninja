What the searches taught us

Problems the log showed, and what changed because of them.

Big playlists stopped at 100 tracks

Spotify cut every playlist at 100 tracks, so [IVY], at tracks 704 to 961 of a 966-track playlist, was never searched. Playlists now read up to 1,000 tracks.

Fixed

Lineups with country codes

A pasted lineup written as "Bassjackers (NL)" matched 1 of 60 names. Tags like (NL) and (live) are ignored now, and 35 of the 60 match.

Fixed

"Nimino" found nothing

ADE lists the artist as "Nimino Banner". The planner now also matches the title of a show with one artist on it.

Fixed

Tidal and SoundCloud links

People pasted links the planner couldn't read. Tidal and Deezer playlists work now. SoundCloud likes are always private.

Fixed for Tidal and Deezer

Typos lose people

"enrico sanguiliano" and "Dave Clark" found nothing. Typed names now get "Did you mean Enrico Sangiuliano?".

Fixed

Daytime searches are genres

"Acid", "Hardstyle" and "House" found nothing. Daytime search now reads ADE's tags and genres, says when a genre is night-only, and has 16 topics.

Fixed

Stars barely got used

2 stars in five days. With a "Save to my plan" button, a week timetable, a map and a story image, stars went to {{STARS_TOTAL}}.

Fixed

Tickets are expensive

ADE publishes no prices. "On a budget" lists free parties and tickets under €25 or €50 (prices read from Weeztix and Resident Advisor on each sync), with a club or laid-back vibe and venue size.

Fixed

Find events is used as a search for everything

On its first day people typed artist names ("Silva bumpa" four times, "Polygonia"), genres ("psy trance", "footwork") and pasted Spotify and YouTube playlists into it. It answered those with 10 popular events, not with that artist or playlist. One box should send a link or a name to artist match, and say so when nothing fits.

Next

Gemini rarely answered

Almost every typed request fell back to keywords: Gemini 3.8 Flash was slow or busy inside the 6 s budget. Keyword reading also missed dates like "the 24th of October".

Next

Big names missing

Chris Lake, Bicep, Fred again.. and Fisher are the most wanted acts not on the lineup: a ready-made "missing from ADE" post.
